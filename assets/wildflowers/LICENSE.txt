Wildflower assets used in INNATE

Both creators publish the following packs under Creative Commons Zero v1.0
Universal (CC0-1.0). This applies only to the assets in this directory;
Sprout Lands artwork retains Cup Nooble's separate license.

BTL games — Pixel art fauna asset pack
Source: https://btl-games.itch.io/pixel-art-fauna-asset-pack
Official download: flowers.zip, itch.io upload ID 3216074
Contains 100 original 16x16 flora sprites, packed unchanged into btl/flowers.png.

karsiori — FREE Pixel Art Flower Pack
Source: https://karsiori.itch.io/free-pixel-art-flower-pack
Official download: Pixel Art Flower Pack.zip, itch.io upload ID 9464396
Contains 13 flower designs with color variants. All loose flower sprites are
packed unchanged into karsiori/flowers.png; pots and bushes are not included.

The catalog records the original source PNG hashes, sizes, and atlas positions.
Atlas packing preserves all source pixels. Some accent sprites are rendered at
half scale with nearest filtering to fit the existing game's pixel scale.
Credits are appreciated by the artists and are retained in docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md.

The meadow/ directory contains INNATE's AI-assisted adaptations of the flower
designs, rendered with a shared palette to fit the game. This is a separate
edited atlas; it does not replace the original CC0 pack sheets. Its source
master and offline conversion process are described in meadow/STYLE.md.
